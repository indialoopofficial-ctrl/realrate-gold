Disclaimer

Important disclaimer for RealRate benchmark gold and silver rates, jewellery calculations, selling estimates and purity tools.

Informational estimates only

RealRate provides benchmark-based information and calculation tools. Nothing on the website is a guaranteed buy price, guaranteed selling payout, investment recommendation, tax advice or professional valuation.

Local prices can differ

Actual gold and silver prices can vary by city, dealer, time, inventory, brand premium and market movement. Jewellery invoices may include making charges, wastage and other disclosed charges. Selling old gold may involve purity testing, melting/refining loss and buyer-specific deductions.

Purity tools are mathematical

Purity and karat converters show mathematical equivalents. They cannot determine the physical purity of an item. Only appropriate testing by a qualified professional can establish actual composition.

Benchmark timing

RealRate attempts to load the latest available benchmark and can use a cached or saved value when a live source is unavailable. Always check the displayed source status and update time. Precious-metal markets can move after the displayed benchmark was published.

GST and calculations

Where the website shows a simple 3% GST estimate, it is intended for comparison and calculator use. The exact tax treatment and invoice amount depend on the actual transaction and applicable rules.

Decision responsibility

Before buying, selling, pledging or valuing precious metals, confirm the final numbers directly with the party handling the transaction. You are responsible for decisions made using information from this website.
