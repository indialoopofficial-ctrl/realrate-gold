Frequently asked questions

Aaj sona bechne ka rate kaise pata chalega?

Relevant purity benchmark se net metal value nikaliye aur buyer deduction ko compare kijiye. RealRate 24K, 22K aur 18K ke example sell estimates dikhata hai.

Jeweller market rate se kam kyon deta hai?

Testing, refining or melting loss, non-gold parts, stones aur buyer margin ki wajah se offered payout benchmark se kam ho sakta hai.

Purana gold bechne se pehle kya karna chahiye?

Weight aur purity test ka result note karein, deduction ko written quote me samjhein aur possible ho to multiple buyers compare karein.
