About RealRate

RealRate ke baare me: benchmark-based gold and silver rate tools, transparent calculations, source methodology and user-first purpose.

What RealRate does

RealRate is an independent informational website built to make common gold and silver calculations easier for users in India. It combines benchmark metal rates with simple tools for 24K, 22K/916 and 18K gold, silver, jewellery bills, old-gold estimates and purity conversion.

Our goal is transparency. Wherever a number is an estimate rather than a guaranteed trade price, we label it as an estimate. Jewellery making charges, wastage, retailer premiums, local taxes or buyer deductions can change the final amount you pay or receive.

Where the benchmark comes from

RealRate currently uses publicly available benchmark information from IBJA as its primary rate reference. The website may use a recently saved benchmark if a live fetch temporarily fails. Source status and update time are shown so users can understand whether a displayed value is live, cached or saved.

Independent information

RealRate is not a jeweller, bullion dealer, exchange, bank or investment adviser. It does not buy or sell precious metals and does not guarantee that a local shop will quote the same price. The calculators are designed for comparison and estimation before a user confirms a transaction with a real seller or buyer.

How we improve the site

We review formulas, internal links, mobile usability and rate-loading behaviour regularly. Pages are written for practical search questions such as “aaj gold ka rate kya hai”, “916 gold rate today”, “old gold selling value” and jewellery bill calculations, while keeping the underlying math visible to the user.
